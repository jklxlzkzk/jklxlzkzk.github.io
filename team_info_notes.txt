UNCERTAINTIES / JUDGMENT CALLS:
1. Marseille league titles = 9 (official). The 1992-93 title was stripped for match-fixing; some
   sources count 10 including it, and some count the 1929 amateur championship. 9 is the official
   Ligue 1/Division 1 figure used by Wikipedia/FFF.
2. Juventus league titles = 36 (official FIGC count). Juventus claim 38 including the revoked
   2004-05 and 2005-06 titles. 36 is the official figure.
3. Bayern "34" = total German championships (33 Bundesliga + 1932 pre-Bundesliga). Some sources say
   "34th Bundesliga title" loosely; total official = 34.
4. Marseille founded = 1892 (as omnisport club; renamed Olympique de Marseille in 1899).
5. Barcelona stadium listed as Camp Nou (currently under renovation; team playing at Estadi Olimpic
   during 2024-26 — Camp Nou remains the official home).
6. Atletico stadium listed as "Metropolitano" (sponsor name changes: Civitas/Riyadh Air Metropolitano).
7. Marseille stadium listed as "Stade Velodrome" (sponsor name: Orange Velodrome).
8. Dortmund stadium listed as "Signal Iduna Park" (also known as Westfalenstadion).
9. Man City Europa League = 0 — corrected during review (never won it).
